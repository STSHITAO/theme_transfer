The visual language is defined by a cold, refreshing atmosphere with a unified pale cyan/blue color palette, regardless of the original icon's color. [Reference Raw To Style Transform]
The transformation converts flat geometry into volumetric 3D objects with significant thickness (extrusion). Original colors are discarded in favor of a uniform 'frozen water' aesthetic (pale blue/cyan). The material becomes semi-transparent glass or ice, allowing light to pass through while maintaining structural integrity. [Stroke Transform Rule]
消除所有 2D 描边。将原本的平面线条转化为具有物理厚度的 3D 实体。边缘不再是锐利的矢量线，而是具有折射率的圆润冰体边缘。

[Composition Transform Rule]
从平面扁平化布局转换为 2.5D 或 3D 立体布局。主体被赋予 Z 轴厚度，通常略微侧转或正视但强调体积。原本位于底部的装饰（如火山版的火焰）可能被移位至顶部悬浮或缩小作为点缀，以避免破坏主体的完整性。

[Subject Scale Rule]
主体在画布中保持较大比例（约占高度的 60-70%），但在垂直方向上可能因为增加了底座或为了容纳立体透视而略微压缩视觉高度。允许为主体增加一个通用的“冰台”底座。
