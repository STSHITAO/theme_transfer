The transformation converts flat, solid-color silhouettes into volumetric 3D sculptures. The original geometry is preserved but extruded to have thickness. [Stroke Transform Rule]

[Composition Transform Rule]

[Detail Complexity Rule]
简化 original 的内部细节（如文字笔画的复杂连接），将其概括为厚实的冰块结构。增加材质层面的细节（气泡、纹理、反光）来替代原本的图形细节。
